About

An accountant who reads a balance sheet like a story.

I'm an experienced Accountant and Financial Analyst with over 14 years of experience, including more than 6 years of Australian accounting and finance experience across financial reporting, bookkeeping, payroll, auditing and financial analysis.

My work spans BAS preparation, general ledger and bank reconciliation, bookkeeping in Xero and QuickBooks, financial statement and working paper preparation, payroll processing, month-end and year-end reporting, and ATO compliance. I recently completed a Master of Analytics and the Certified Financial Analytics Professional (CFAP) credential, and work confidently across Xero, MYOB, QuickBooks, SQL, Power BI and advanced Excel.

I bring careful attention to detail, confidentiality and accuracy, along with strong stakeholder engagement, problem-solving and decision-making — the combination that turns financial data into something a business can actually act on.
